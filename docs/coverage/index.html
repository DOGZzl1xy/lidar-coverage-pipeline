How the years were set and checked

  • Footprints: 2,201 collections in the hobuinc/usgs-lidar inventory plus 593 work units from the official USGS 3DEP index that the inventory lacks, 2,794 in CONUS. Retrieved October 6, 2026.
  • Year of a collection: its latest acquisition year, taken in this order: a reviewed date (62 collections, each with evidence in the code), the official 3DEP collection end date, then the year in the collection name. A collection whose flights cross the cutoff counts as at or after it.
  • Date check: GPS times sampled from the point clouds of all collections. 2,286 gave measured dates, and 98.4% of those with official dates fall in the same years. Ten collections fell on the other side of 2015; eight were corrected. The 578 collections without usable timestamps (mostly 2006 to 2012) keep their official or name year.
  • Area figures: footprints rasterized at 1 km in EPSG:5070 and intersected with Census 2024 county subdivisions (water placeholders excluded). Percentages are of total subdivision area, including inland water. The memo's gap counts use exact polygons and a 5% threshold per subdivision.
